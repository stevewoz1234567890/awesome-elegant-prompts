## Note

This is motivational framing. Treat outputs as ideas to test, not truths.

## Prompts

1. **7-Day Mental Training Program**

```text
Act as a neuroplasticity-informed coach (not a clinician). Design a 7-day mental training program to boost my [skill: memory/critical thinking/creativity/etc].

Include:
- daily exercises rooted in science
- thought experiments
- a way to measure progress

Make it unconventional yet practical.
```

2. **Master Problem-Solving**

```text
Become a world-class strategist. I’m facing [specific challenge]. Analyze the situation, break it into actionable steps, and provide a detailed plan.

Include:
- potential obstacles
- creative workarounds
- real-world examples
```

3. **Superhuman Foresight**

```text
Transform into a futurist. Predict three major trends that will shape [field] over the next decade.

Base this on current signals and logical extrapolation. For each trend:
- explain implications
- how I can prepare or benefit
```

4. **Boost Persuasion Skills**

```text
Become a master rhetorician. Help me craft an argument to convince [audience] that [idea].

Provide:
- a compelling narrative
- emotional hooks
- data-driven points

Then anticipate 3 counterarguments and refute them.
```

5. **Learn 10x Faster**

```text
Act as a personal tutor with infinite patience. Teach me [topic] as a beginner.

Break it into bite-sized lessons, use everyday analogies, and suggest practical exercises to master it quickly.
```

6. **Enhance Self-Awareness**

```text
Act as a psychological mirror (not a therapist). Based on my input:
[describe a recent decision or behavior]

Analyze:
- thought patterns
- motivations
- blind spots

Offer 3 actionable steps to improve decision-making and emotional resilience.
```

7. **Optimize Daily Performance**

```text
Turn into a peak-performance coach. Design a personalized 24-hour schedule to maximize my energy, focus, and output.

My constraints: [night owl/9-5/kids/etc]
Goal: [goal]

Include breaks and habits that are realistic.
```

8. **Decode a Big Question**

```text
Transform into a curious philosopher. Explain [mystery/topic] using a mix of science, logic, and speculative ideas.

Then suggest a personal experiment or reflection exercise to explore it further.
```

9. **Negotiate Like an Expert**

```text
Act as a negotiation coach. I need to navigate [situation].

Create:
- a step-by-step plan
- a script with exact phrases
- contingency plans for resistance

Also list 3 mistakes to avoid.
```

10. **5-Year Vision**

```text
Act as a life-planning coach. Interview me using 5–7 questions about:
- my biggest dream
- what's holding me back
- my ideal day
- my constraints

Then construct a 5-year plan with milestones and the first step I should take this week.
```
