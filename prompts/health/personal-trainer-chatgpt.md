## Safety note

Not medical advice. If you have injuries or conditions, consult a professional before changing diet/training.

## Prompts

1. **Body Analysis & Goal Setting**

```text
Here’s my current weight: [kg], height: [cm], age: [age], sex: [M/F], and my goal: [lose fat/gain muscle/etc].

Act as a personal trainer and nutrition coach. Create a realistic 12-week fitness + nutrition plan that works without gym access.
Include: weekly schedule, progression, and a simple measurement plan.
```

2. **Customized Weekly Meal Plan**

```text
Design a 7-day meal plan based on [calories] calories/day, [protein]g+ protein, minimal processed carbs, and affordable easy-to-cook ingredients.

Include macros and a grocery list.
Foods I avoid: [list]
```

3. **Fat-Loss Home Workouts**

```text
Build a 4-day weekly workout plan I can do at home with no equipment.

Focus on fat loss, bodyweight strength, and mobility. Each session 25–35 minutes and progressively harder over 12 weeks.
My current level: [beginner/intermediate]
```

4. **Craving & Emotional Eating Support**

```text
Give me 10 high-volume, low-calorie snacks under 200 calories that curb sugar cravings.
Also write a short script I can repeat when I feel the urge to binge.
My constraints/allergies: [list]
```

5. **Habit Tracking System**

```text
Act as a fitness accountability coach. Ask me 5 daily check-in questions (workout, meals, steps, sleep, mood).
Format it as a habit tracker I can fill in every night for 30 days.
```

6. **Mindset & Consistency Journal**

```text
Create a morning journaling script I can complete in under 5 minutes that includes:
1) goal reminder
2) one action I’ll take today
3) gratitude
4) a fitness affirmation
```

7. **Weekly Review + Adjustments**

```text
Every Sunday, help me review my weight, waist measurement, and consistency score (1–10).
Then suggest how to adjust workouts or meals for the next 7 days based on results.
```
