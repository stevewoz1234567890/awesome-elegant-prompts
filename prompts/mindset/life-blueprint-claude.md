## Note

These prompts mix planning, habits, money, and health framing. They are **not** medical, mental-health, or investment advice—treat outputs as drafts to sanity-check with qualified professionals and your own research.

## Prompts

1. **Life Priority Framework**

```text
Create my Life Priority Framework using the 6-category Personal Performance model: Personal Productivity, Personal Resilience, Well-being, Happiness, Longevity, Affluence. Generate baseline metrics for each based on my input, set quarterly KPIs, and provide a monthly scoring template out of 100. Consider my current age of [number], career stage of [description], and relationship status of [description].
```

2. **Relationship Builder Protocol**

```text
Design a Relationship Builder Protocol using Gottman's Sound Relationship House. Include a self-assessment for my 5:1 positive-to-negative ratio, the Gentle Start-Up formula template, and a 20-minute self-soothing protocol for when flooded (heart rate above 100 BPM). Provide conflict management scripts and a weekly friendship-building checklist. Consider my current relationship dynamics of [description] and available time together of [hours weekly].
```

3. **Income Stream Mapper**

```text
Build my Income Stream Mapper using 2026 outlook data. Map 5 income streams ranked by diversification value: international equities, emerging markets, securitized assets, dividend stocks, options strategies. Include an AI infrastructure allocation considering $500B data center spend and $5-8T projected through 2030. Consider my current capital of [amount], risk tolerance of [description], and existing holdings of [description].
```

4. **Health Optimization Engine**

```text
Create my Health Optimization Engine using Shawn Wells' healthspan protocol. Design a sleep schedule with 8:30 PM target, exercise snacks (5-minute movement every waking hour), and supplement stack for Ergothioneine, Dileucine, and Dihydroberberine (86% GLP-1 boost). Include wearable integration for HRV and glucose tracking. Consider my current sleep patterns of [description], fitness level of [description], and health constraints of [limitation].
```

5. **Purpose Clarifier Model**

```text
Develop my Purpose Clarifier Model using Ikigai and Frankl's logotherapy. Map my personal purpose across four levers: The Roots (values), The Trunk (mindset), Supporting Branches (relationships), Branches and Leaves (communication). Generate my purpose statement and a weekly reflection template. Consider my current career of [description], values of [list], and life stage of [description].
```

6. **Daily Routine Architect**

```text
Architect my Daily Routine using Three Work Modes: Maker Mode (deep execution), Connector Mode (communication), Strategist Mode (decisions). Map my chronotype to peak energy windows, design my Golden Hours block, and create a Right to Disconnect protocol. Include a weekly review template. Consider my current work style of [description], chronotype of [morning/night], and typical hours of [timeframe].
```

7. **Goal Tracking System**

```text
Build my Goal Tracking System using OKRs with confidence scores. Structure quarterly objectives across 6 life categories with weekly check-ins tracking: progress, confidence score (0-10), blocker, and help needed. Flag at-risk goals when confidence drops below 50% or stalls for 2 weeks. Consider my current goal clarity of [description] and tracking habits of [description].
```

8. **Stress Management Blueprint**

```text
Create my Stress Management Blueprint using Polyvagal Theory. Map my Window of Tolerance with hyperarousal signs (fight/flight) and hypoarousal signs (freeze). Design vagal toning micro-practices: humming, cold water splash, gargling, extended exhales (4 in, 8 out). Include box breathing (4-4-4-4) and 20-minute self-soothing protocol. Consider my current stress patterns of [description] and triggers of [list].
```
