## Prompts

1. **Outcome-First Language Plan**

```text
My goal is to use [language] confidently for [travel/work/daily conversations].

Create a 30-day plan focused on speaking, listening, and understanding real conversations.
Avoid streaks, games, or textbook-style lessons. Prioritize practical use.
```

2. **High-Frequency Core Vocabulary**

```text
Teach me the most commonly used words and phrases in [language] for [context].

Include:
- pronunciation guidance
- how natives shorten/alter them in speech
- one natural real-life example each
```

3. **Natural Sentence Formation**

```text
Help me build simple, natural sentences people actually use.
Start with short structures and gradually expand them.

Explain word order and meaning in plain language (no jargon).
Language: [language]
```

4. **Real-World Conversation Practice**

```text
Role-play a realistic conversation for [situation] in [language].

Rules:
- speak naturally
- pause after each message so I can respond
- correct my mistakes gently and explain what sounds unnatural
```

5. **Casual vs Formal**

```text
Show how the same idea is expressed casually, neutrally, and formally in [language].

Explain when and why a native speaker would choose each in real life.
Idea: [paste]
```

6. **Common Beginner Mistakes**

```text
List the most common mistakes learners make in everyday conversations in [language].

For each:
- incorrect example
- corrected version
- why the mistake happens
```

7. **Thinking in the Language**

```text
Help me stop translating from English.

Explain how a native speaker would naturally think and structure this idea in [language] instead.
Idea: [paste]
```

8. **Pronunciation Without Audio**

```text
Explain how to pronounce these words without audio using mouth position, rhythm, stress, and pacing.
Words: [list]
Language: [language]
```

9. **Everyday Phrases Natives Use**

```text
Teach everyday phrases natives actually use for [context] in [language].

Include:
- informal shortcuts
- filler words
- fast-speech variations
```

10. **Grammar Without Jargon**

```text
Explain this grammar concept using plain language only.
Avoid technical terms; focus on how people actually use it when speaking.
Concept: [paste]
Language: [language]
```
