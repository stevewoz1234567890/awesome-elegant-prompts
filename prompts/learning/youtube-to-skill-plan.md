## Prompts

1. **Comprehensive Overview**

```text
I’ve shared a YouTube video. Give me a high-level overview of everything being taught, broken into the main skills, concepts, and phases, as if you’re introducing it to a beginner.
Video transcript/summary: [paste or link]
```

2. **Skill Tree Extraction**

```text
From this video, identify the complete skill tree required to master the topic. Organize it from foundational skills to advanced execution.
Video transcript/summary: [paste]
```

3. **Actionable Learning Plan**

```text
Turn this video into a structured learning plan with clear practice steps. Assume I want to go from zero to competent as efficiently as possible.
Constraints: [time/week, tools, budget]
```

4. **Core Concepts & Mental Models**

```text
Extract the core concepts and mental models explained in this video. Explain each one simply and show how it’s used in practice.
```

5. **Practical Application Focus**

```text
Based on this video, list the specific actions I should take to apply what’s being taught in real-world scenarios. Avoid theory and focus on execution.
```

6. **Knowledge Check & Evaluation**

```text
Create questions or exercises based on this video that test whether someone truly understands the material, not just remembers it.
Include an answer key or evaluation rubric.
```

7. **Workflow & Process Mapping**

```text
Extract any workflows, processes, or step-by-step methods mentioned in this video and reorganize them into the most logical execution order.
Output as a checklist.
```

8. **Common Mistakes & Pitfalls**

```text
Identify common mistakes beginners make when following the advice in this video and explain how to avoid each one.
```
