## Prompts

Money is everywhere. Most people just don’t know where to look.

AI doesn’t replace effort, It redirects it.

Here are 7 prompts to turn ChatGPT into a quiet money engine:

1. **Hidden Demand Finder**

```text
List problems people are actively paying to solve right now. Focus on online, AI-friendly opportunities with low startup costs.
```

2. **Skill Monetization Map**

```text
Take my current skills or interests [list them] and show me how each can be turned into a simple paid offer.
```

3. **Faceless Offer Creator**

```text
Help me create a faceless digital product or service that solves one clear problem. Keep it simple and fast to launch.
```

4. **Pricing Without Guesswork**

```text
Suggest pricing for this offer based on perceived value, not time spent. Include a low-risk entry option.
```

5. **Traffic Without Ads**

```text
List free traffic sources where people are already searching for solutions to this problem.
```

6. **Automation Builder**

```text
Show me how to automate delivery, follow-ups, and basic support so this runs with minimal daily input.
```

7. **Scale the Smart Way**

```text
Create a simple plan to scale this income using systems and repetition, not more hours.
```
