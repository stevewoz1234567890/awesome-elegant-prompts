## Prompts

1. **Viral Video Ideas**

```text
I want to create a YouTube channel about [niche].

Generate 10 video ideas that could go viral based on:
- current trends
- hot topics
- what’s working in my niche

For each idea include: title, thumbnail concept, and why it will click.
```

2. **Viral Video Script**

```text
Write a YouTube video script for [topic].

Include:
- a strong hook (first 10 seconds)
- an interesting story structure to keep retention
- clear sections with timestamps
- a persuasive call to action
```

3. **Faceless Channel Workflow**

```text
I want to start a faceless YouTube channel in [niche].

Show me a workflow for:
- writing scripts with an LLM
- AI voice narration options (pros/cons)
- sourcing legal stock footage
- editing tools and a repeatable template

Goal: consistency and quality, not spam.
```

4. **YouTube SEO (Title/Description/Tags)**

```text
Create a high-ranking YouTube title, description, and tags for my video on [video topic].

Optimize for:
- search intent
- curiosity (without clickbait)
- clear keywords

Also give 3 alternate titles for A/B testing.
```

5. **Irresistible Hook**

```text
Help me craft a catchy 15-second intro for my YouTube video on [topic].

Make it attention-grabbing and specific. Provide 5 variations with different tones.
```

6. **30-Day Growth Strategy**

```text
I'm launching a new YouTube channel about [niche].

Create a 30-day plan:
- upload schedule
- 12–20 video ideas
- packaging checklist (title/thumbnail)
- distribution plan (shorts, communities, etc.)
- basic monetization milestones
```

7. **Repurpose Content**

```text
I have a YouTube video about [topic].

Show me how to repurpose it into:
- a blog post
- an Instagram Reel
- a TikTok
- a Threads/X post

Include a lightweight workflow so I can do it weekly.
```
