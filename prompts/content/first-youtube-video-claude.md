## When to use

Use these prompts when you are stuck in analysis paralysis, planning your very first YouTube video, or wanting to simplify and optimize your recording and launch workflow without professional gear.

## Prompts

### 1. The "Impulse-Topic" Friction Killer

For isolating a single dead-simple topic that requires no script memorization and can be filmed immediately.

```text
Act as an Elite YouTube Growth Strategist. I am completely stuck in analysis paralysis and terrified of pressing record. Help me isolate 1 dead-simple, high-intent topic based on [Niche] that requires absolutely zero deep research or script memorization. Map out a strict, low-stress 5-minute talking point layout based entirely on my raw personal experiences and direct daily observations.
```

### 2. The "Faceless-Fallback" Hybrid Script

To minimize on-camera pressure by limiting face-to-camera time to just the introduction and conclusion.

```text
Act as an Expert Video Script Engineer. Write an ultra-short, 2-minute script layout discussing [Topic] designed to minimize on-camera pressure. Re-architect the classic format into a 'Hybrid-Delivery' framework: I only need to show my face for a 10-second introduction and a 10-second conclusion. The remaining middle section must rely entirely on scannable, text-based on-screen slides. No fluff.
```

### 3. The "Pattern-Interrupt" Hook Matrix

To craft high-retention video hooks that grab attention and stop scrolling in the first five seconds.

```text
Act as a Viral YouTube Copywriter. Generate 15 high-retention video opener scripts tailored specifically to [Audience]. Focus entirely on sharing case studies, rapid process breakdowns, and data-backed industry insights. Every single hook must utilize a sharp structural pattern-interrupt layout explicitly engineered to freeze viewers instantly within the first 5 seconds of playback.
```

### 4. The "Zero-Gear" Studio Configuration

To set up a high-quality, professional filming environment using only your phone and natural lighting.

```text
Act as a Professional Cinematic Director of Photography. Write a comprehensive Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) to configure a crisp, high-authority filming layout using only a standard smartphone and natural window light. Eliminate all technical friction loops: detail the exact framing rules, phone height placements, background declutter steps, and audio positioning thresholds for beginners.
```

### 5. The "Improvisational" Teleprompter SOP

To speak naturally and maintain authentic eye contact with the lens without reading a script word-for-word.

```text
Act as a World-Class Public Speaking Coach. Create a step-by-step Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) to deliver my video text naturally without reading word-for-word from a screen. Teach me the 'Bullet-to-Speech' iteration framework layout to rapidly internalize 3 core concept milestones, eliminate verbal hesitation ticks, and maintain authentic, peer-to-peer eye contact with the camera lens.
```

### 6. The "Single-Take" Delivery Protocol

To establish a fast-paced recording workflow where mistakes are expected and easily marked for editing.

```text
Act as an Elite Post-Production Film Supervisor. Design a high-velocity, low-friction filming workflow engineered to bypass the fear of making mistakes on camera. Teach me the 'Continuous-Roll' execution protocol where I never press stop when I mess up a line. Detail the exact visual and audio clapping cues to layout easy editing markers, turning a messy 20-minute recording sprint into a clean file.
```

### 7. The "Micro-Asset" Distribution Funnel

To write descriptions and pinned comments optimized to drive traffic to high-value leads or lead magnets.

```text
Act as an Inbound Conversational Funnel Engineer. Write a high-converting script layout for my video's description and pinned comment section. Optimize the top 3 lines of text above the fold to capture immediate clicks for a free, tracking-linked digital asset. Integrate 5 long-tail semantic keywords naturally and use command-driven micro-copy to force high description click velocity.
```

### 8. The "Velocity-Check" Launch Audit

To analyze the performance of your first upload over its initial 48 hours and identify bottlenecks.

```text
Act as a Data-Driven YouTube Operations Auditor. My first uploaded video performance metrics over its initial 48 hours are: [Impressions] views, [CTR]% click rate, [Retention]% average duration, and [Subscribers] net channel growth. Diagnose the exact script formatting, visual title layout, or topic positioning bottleneck stalling my publishing velocity. Provide a strict checklist.
```
