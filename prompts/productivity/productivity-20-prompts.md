## Prompts

1. **Getting Things Done (GTD)**

```text
I’ve got notes, emails, and tasks scattered everywhere. Help me create a GTD-style system to collect, clarify, and sort them into next actions, projects, or reference.
My tools: [Notion/Google Docs/Apple Notes/etc]
My constraints: [time, team, etc]
```

2. **Pomodoro Breakdown**

```text
I need to finish [task] today. Break it into four 25-minute Pomodoros with clear focus points and what to do during each break.
```

3. **Eat The Frog**

```text
I’ve been putting off [task]. Break it into manageable steps and schedule it as the first thing I do tomorrow. Include a 5-minute “start ritual”.
```

4. **Time Blocking**

```text
Plan my workday from [start] to [end] with time blocks for deep work, meetings, admin, and breaks. Prioritize creative work in the morning.
Constraints: [meetings, commute, etc]
```

5. **Parkinson’s Law**

```text
I usually spend [time] on [task]. Create a tight [new time] structure with specific time budgets for drafting, editing, and formatting.
```

6. **80/20 Rule**

```text
Here’s my task list for this week: [paste tasks]. Show me the top 20% that will give the biggest results and suggest what to defer or skip.
```

7. **Ivy Lee Method**

```text
Based on my current goals, help me pick and rank 6 tasks for tomorrow. Rule: only move to the next once the previous is complete.
Goals: [paste]
Backlog: [paste]
```

8. **Most Important Task (MIT)**

```text
I’ve got 3 key things tomorrow: [A], [B], [C]. Which should be my MIT and why? Propose a 90-minute deep-work block for it.
```

9. **Zeigarnik Effect (Finish One Thing)**

```text
I have half-done projects piling up: [list]. Help me choose one to finish today to reduce mental clutter. Make a “definition of done”.
```

10. **2-Minute Rule**

```text
Here’s a list of small tasks: [paste]. Tell me which ones are under 2 minutes and group them into a “Quick Wins” batch I can do now.
```

11. **Time Tracking Setup**

```text
Help me track how I use my time this week. Create 5 categories (deep work, admin, meetings, distractions, breaks) and a daily log template.
```

12. **Daily Highlight**

```text
Tomorrow’s packed with meetings. Suggest one meaningful highlight task I can fit in that would still make the day feel productive.
My goals: [paste]
```

13. **SMART Goal**

```text
I want to [goal]. Turn this into a SMART goal with numbers, timeline, and a weekly plan.
```

14. **WOOP**

```text
I want to [habit]. Use WOOP (Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan) to help me make it happen. Include “if-then” plans for tired/distracted days.
```

15. **Don’t Break the Chain**

```text
I want to do [habit] daily for [N] days. Create a simple streak tracker and a rule for what to do if I miss a day.
```

16. **Bullet Journal Starter**

```text
Help me start a bullet journal with an index, monthly log, and daily log for [month]. Include page setup ideas and 5 example entries.
```

17. **10-Minute Morning Routine**

```text
Build me a 10-minute morning routine that stacks journaling, water, planning, and movement. Use clear triggers and an exact order.
```

18. **Eisenhower Matrix**

```text
Here are [N] things I need to do this week: [paste list]. Put them into the Eisenhower Matrix and suggest what to drop or delegate.
```

19. **Task Batching**

```text
I have writing, admin, and outreach tasks today. Organize them into three batches and propose a schedule that protects focus.
```

20. **Weekly Review**

```text
Guide me through a Friday review:
- what went well
- what I didn’t finish (and why)
- what to stop/start/continue

Then help me set top priorities for next week based on current projects: [paste]
```
