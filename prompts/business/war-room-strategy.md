## Prompts

1. **Corporate Wargamer (Red Team)**

```text
Act as a ruthless corporate strategist and game theorist. I am currently [describe situation/business model]. Conduct a Red Team exercise against me.

Output:
1) My 3 biggest blind spots
2) How a competitor would kill my business in 6 months
3) The counter-strategy to bulletproof my position

Be direct and specific.
```

2. **Black Swan Negotiator**

```text
You are a hostage negotiator trained by Chris Voss and a high-stakes corporate closer. I am about to enter a negotiation with [person/role] about [deal].

Simulate the conversation (they push back hard). After each of my replies, coach me on:
- Tactical empathy
- Labeling techniques
- No-oriented questions

Goal: reach my outcome without escalating conflict.
```

3. **First Principles Shredder**

```text
Adopt the mindset of a first-principles thinker. I am facing this problem: [problem].

Do not give the standard solution. First:
1) list the fundamental truths/constraints that cannot be changed
2) identify the biggest hidden assumptions
3) rebuild a solution from scratch that is 10x cheaper or faster

Format the final answer as a plan with milestones.
```

4. **Multiverse Decision Matrix**

```text
You are a decision architect. I must choose between:
- Option A: [A]
- Option B: [B]

Run a second-order thinking analysis. Project consequences across:
- 1 week
- 1 year
- 10 years

Then apply a regret minimization framework: which will I least regret at age 80?
Quantify risk vs reward with a simple scoring table.
```

5. **Impossible Outreach Architect**

```text
You are a world-class copywriter. I need a response from [name/role], who is extremely busy.

Draft a pattern-interrupt email:
- Under 75 words
- Subject line that is hard to ignore (not clickbait)
- Offer value upfront
- Clear call to action

Use public context about them (I’ll paste it below) to tailor the hook.
Context: [paste]
```

6. **Ruthless Essentialist**

```text
You are an efficiency consultant. Audit my to-do list: [paste list].

Apply:
- Pareto Principle (80/20)
- Eisenhower Matrix

Output:
1) Delete ~50%
2) Delegate ~30% (suggest who/what)
3) Identify the single domino task that makes the rest easier/irrelevant
4) A 2-hour deep work protocol to finish that domino task today
```

7. **Audience Mind-Reader**

```text
You are a behavioral psychologist and viral data scientist. Analyze the topic: [topic].

Do not write generic content. Map:
- fears
- secret desires
- unspoken biases

Then generate 5 counter-intuitive angles that validate feelings but challenge worldview.
```
