## Note

These prompts assume you’re using Gemini features that can pull current web signals. Always sanity-check results and comply with platform rules.

## Prompts

1. **Skill-to-Demand Analyzer**

```text
Analyze my skills: [list 5–7 skills], my availability: [X hours/week], and my interests: [list 3–5].

Using current Google search trends, suggest 10 side hustle ideas ranked by:
- Search demand
- Monetization speed
- Competition level

For each idea: give a 1-sentence offer and a first-week plan.
```

2. **Real Market Proof Check**

```text
I want to start: [side hustle idea].

Validate it using Google-visible data:
- what people are searching related to this
- who is already making money (examples)
- what users complain about in reviews/forums/comments
- whether demand is increasing or declining

End with a go/no-go recommendation and why.
```

3. **SEO + Competition Edge**

```text
My idea: [describe idea].

Analyze:
- top-ranking competitors
- keywords they dominate
- gaps they are missing
- how I can position differently (UX, pricing, speed, niche)

Output a positioning statement + top 10 keywords to target.
```

4. **Offer That Converts Search Traffic**

```text
Turn [my idea] into an offer optimized for search traffic.

Create:
- a beginner offer ($29–$99)
- a core offer ($299–$999)
- a premium service/bundle

Explain what problem each solves and why users would pay.
```

5. **Search Intent Customer Avatar**

```text
I sell: [product/service].

Build my ideal customer profile based on search intent:
- what they type into Google at each stage (awareness → consideration → purchase)
- pain points behind those searches
- buying triggers
- trust signals they look for
```

6. **30-Day Launch Using Google Ecosystem**

```text
I want to make my first $1,000 in 30 days with: [idea].

Create a step-by-step plan using:
- Google Search
- YouTube
- Google Docs/Sites
- free distribution channels

Keep it lean and beginner-friendly. Include daily actions and a simple KPI.
```

7. **Search-Driven Content Engine**

```text
Create a 30-day content plan driven by current search demand for: [niche/idea].

Include:
- blog/post ideas
- YouTube topics
- short-form angles
- keywords with buyer intent
- simple CTAs to convert traffic
```

8. **Scale With Evergreen Traffic**

```text
Assume I'm earning my first $1K/month from: [idea].

Show me:
- how to rank long-term
- what content compounds over time
- monetization upgrades
- how to scale to $5K/month without daily posting
```
