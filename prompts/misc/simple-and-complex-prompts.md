## Simple prompts

- Little interpretation required
- Single task
- Easily categorized
- Ask about things that appear widely on the internet
- Require little deep context (cultural, scientific, linguistic, etc.)

## Complex prompts

- Ambiguous or very nuanced language
- Multiple steps, many interdependent steps
- Multiple categories or an unusual ask
- Ask about things that appear in niche sources and are unlikely to show up often in the training set

## Deeply embedded in a rich context

The ability of LLMs to respond to complex prompts is limited by their training set. But remember: your prompts also determine the model’s output—with the right prompt design, LLMs can carry out surprisingly complex tasks.
