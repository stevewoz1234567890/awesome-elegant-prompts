## When to use

Use this guide when configuring a new ChatGPT account or auditing your current settings. Five minutes of setup can save you dozens of hours of repetitive work over the next year by tailoring ChatGPT to your voice, workflow, and privacy preferences.

## 7 Settings to Configure Today

### 1. Customize how ChatGPT talks (Custom Instructions)

Tired of generic, robotic, or overly verbose responses? Set up Custom Instructions to define your baseline context.

- **Where to find it**: `Settings` → `Personalization` → `Custom Instructions`
- **What to tell ChatGPT**:
  - **Your Profile**: Your goals, your profession, and the context of your daily work.
  - **Preferred Style**: Your preferred writing style (e.g., professional, casual, plain-language).
  - **Response Format**: How concise or detailed you want answers to be by default (e.g., "no fluff," "always start with bullet points").

### 2. Turn on Memory

Stop repeating yourself at the beginning of every chat. Memory allows ChatGPT to carry context across different sessions seamlessly.

- **Where to find it**: `Settings` → `Personalization` → `Memory`
- **What to enable**: Turn on **Reference Saved Memories** and **Reference Chat History**.
- **How it helps**: ChatGPT will automatically remember your active projects, specific tools you use, preferences, and recurring context.

### 3. Protect your conversations (Privacy controls)

By default, OpenAI may use your conversations to train their models. Protect your sensitive personal and professional data.

- **Where to find it**: `Settings` → `Data Controls`
- **What to do**: Turn **OFF** "Improve the model for everyone".

### 4. Unlock more models

Ensure you always have access to the latest and most specialized models for different tasks.

- **Where to find it**: `Settings` → `General`
- **What to do**: Enable **Show additional models**. This makes it easy to switch to the best-performing model for coding, reasoning, or fast drafting when they are rolled out.

### 5. Choose a personality

Fine-tune the tone of your AI assistant to fit your daily style.

- **Where to find it**: `Settings` → `Personalization` → `Personality`
- **What to choose**: Set a tone that matches your communication needs (e.g., Professional, Warm, Concise, or Thoughtful). A small change here makes a massive difference in day-to-day engagement.

### 6. Connect your tools

Bring ChatGPT into your actual workflow instead of letting it live in an isolated window.

- **Where to find it**: `Settings` → `Apps & Connectors`
- **What to connect**: Integrate services such as your Calendars, Email, cloud storage, and team productivity tools to allow direct file access and scheduling capability.

### 7. Use scheduled tasks

Automate recurring, low-value work so you don't have to trigger it manually every day.

- **Where to find it**: Look for **Scheduled Tasks** or automation features under your platform workflow settings.
- **What to automate**:
  - Daily news summaries from specific sources.
  - Weekly project check-ins or status reports.
  - Competitor or research monitoring.
  - Re-running custom prompts at set intervals.

## Pro-Tips for Continued Optimization

- **Audit regularly**: Review your saved memories (`Settings` → `Personalization` → `Manage Memory`) every month. Delete any outdated, incorrect, or temporary constraints that ChatGPT might have saved.
- **Keep Custom Instructions updated**: As your role or focus changes, take 2 minutes to update your instructions.
- **Separate personal and professional spaces**: If you use ChatGPT for both work and personal tasks, explicitly state in your Custom Instructions how to handle different types of requests.
