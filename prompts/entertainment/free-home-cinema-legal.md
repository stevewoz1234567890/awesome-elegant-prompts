## Note

Focus on **legal** streaming sources. Avoid piracy.

## Prompts

1. **Find Free Legal Streaming Sites**

```text
Give me a list of safe, legal websites where I can watch movies and TV shows for free.
Organize them by quality, genre, and country availability.
Country: [your country]
```

2. **Access Global Content**

```text
Recommend free platforms that offer international films and series with English subtitles.
Prioritize high-quality options.
Country: [your country]
Genres I like: [list]
```

3. **Discover New Movies**

```text
List 20 high-quality movies from the last 5 years that I can watch legally for free.
Country: [your country]
Preferred genres: [list]
```

4. **Hidden Gems Finder**

```text
Recommend underrated but high-quality films and shows available on free legal platforms.
Country: [your country]
```

5. **Genre-Based Recommendations**

```text
Create a list of free legal movies sorted by genre (action, horror, sci-fi, romance, etc.) and include a short description for each.
Country: [your country]
```

6. **Build a Free Watchlist**

```text
Generate a curated watchlist of movies and series available on free streaming services, optimized for my taste preferences.
My tastes: [paste]
Country: [your country]
```

7. **Improve Streaming Quality**

```text
Suggest tools, browser extensions, or settings that improve video quality, subtitles, and playback on legal streaming platforms.
Device: [laptop/TV/phone]
Browser/app: [Chrome/Firefox/etc]
```
