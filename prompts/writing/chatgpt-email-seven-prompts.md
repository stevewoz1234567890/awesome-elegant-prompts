## Hook

Stop saying to ChatGPT "Write me an email"! Bad request = bad result.

Use these prompts instead and you'll see the magic:

## Prompts

1. **Professional Email Writer**

```text
Act as a first-class communications specialist. Rewrite this email to make it professional, clear, concise, and polite while preserving my original intent. Improve the tone, structure, grammar, and overall flow of the text. My email: [paste the email].
```

2. **The Perfect Cold Email**

```text
Write a short, persuasive, and highly impactful cold email for this purpose: [write the purpose]. Include a strong opening that grabs attention, a clear value proposition, credibility, and a simple call to action (CTA). Make it feel natural, confident, and not like a direct sales pitch.
```

3. **Companies' Official Response**

```text
Write a professional reply to this email I received: [paste the email]. Keep the tone respectful, reply to each point clearly, and write a response that strengthens trust and communication.
```

4. **The Apology Email**

```text
Write a sincere apology email in a mature tone for this situation: [describe the situation]. Take responsibility, express your genuine understanding, suggest a step to correct the mistake, and offer a positive way to move forward.
```

5. **The follow-up that gets a reply**

```text
Write a polite and effective follow-up email in which you remind [write the person's name] about [write the topic]. Make it respectful, not pushy, and clear. And include a simple request (CTA) that encourages a response.
```

6. **Email Simplification**

```text
Rewrite my email to make it shorter, clearer, and easier to understand while maintaining professionalism and respect. Remove unnecessary words but increase the impact's strength. The email: [paste the text].
```

7. **The Perfect Sales Email**

```text
Write a high-converting sales email for my product/service: [describe the product]. Make sure it has a strong opening that grabs attention, emotional benefits, social proof, a clear explanation, and a powerful call to action (CTA). And keep it in a conversational style that focuses on value, not a pushy one.
```
