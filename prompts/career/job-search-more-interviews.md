## Prompts

1. **Resume Optimization**

```text
Revise my resume for [JOB ROLE]. Highlight quantifiable achievements, strong action verbs, and keywords recruiters search for.

Inputs:
- Resume: [paste]
- Target job description: [paste]

Output:
- Updated resume bullets
- A keyword checklist I can verify against the job description
```

2. **Cover Letter Creation**

```text
Write a concise, confident cover letter for [JOB ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Make it personalized, professional, and tailored to this position.

Inputs:
- Job description: [paste]
- My experience highlights: [paste]
```

3. **LinkedIn Profile Enhancement**

```text
Rewrite my LinkedIn About section to position me as a top candidate for [JOB ROLE]. Focus on credibility, clarity, and authentic personality.

Current About: [paste]
```

4. **Interview Preparation**

```text
Generate 10 common interview questions for [JOB ROLE] with natural, confident answers that sound conversational (not scripted).

My background: [paste key points]
```

5. **Skill Alignment (Pivot)**

```text
Reframe my experience in [INDUSTRY] to align with [NEW INDUSTRY OR ROLE]. Highlight transferable skills and measurable impact.

My experience: [paste]
Target role: [paste]
```

6. **Recruiter Outreach**

```text
Write a brief LinkedIn message to a recruiter hiring for [JOB ROLE]. Keep it warm, concise, and compelling with a clear call to action.

My value proposition: [1–2 sentences]
```

7. **Follow-Up Messaging**

```text
Create a follow-up email after an interview. Make it courteous, memorable, and clearly show why I'm a strong fit.

Company: [name]
Role: [role]
Interview topics: [bullets]
```
