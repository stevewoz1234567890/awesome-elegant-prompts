## How to prompt AI in 2026

### CHATBOT – R.T.C.R.O.S.

- **R – Role**
- **T – Task**
- **C – Context**
- **R – Reasoning**
- **O – Output**
- **S – Stop**

### IMAGE – F.R.A.M.E.

- **F – Focus**
- **R – Rendering**
- **A – Attributes**
- **M – Medium and Light**
- **E – Environment**

### VIDEO – S.A.M.E.

- **S – Scene**
- **A – Actor**
- **M – Motion**
- **E – Edges of Time**

## Templates

### Chatbot (R.T.C.R.O.S.)

```text
Role: [who the model should be]
Task: [what you want done]
Context: [inputs, constraints, audience, examples, tools you can/can't use]
Reasoning: [ask for a short justification + assumptions + checks]
Output: [format, sections, length, tone, and any required tables/lists]
Stop: [when to stop, what not to do, what to ask if unclear]
```

### Image (F.R.A.M.E.)

```text
Focus: [main subject + composition + camera/shot]
Rendering: [style, fidelity, lens, realism vs stylized, resolution cues]
Attributes: [materials, colors, textures, details, keywords]
Medium and Light: [medium (photo/3D/illustration) + lighting + mood]
Environment: [setting, background, atmosphere, time period, context]
```

### Video (S.A.M.E.)

```text
Scene: [where/when, establishing details, mood]
Actor: [subject(s), wardrobe/props, defining traits]
Motion: [camera movement + subject movement + pacing]
Edges of Time: [duration, cuts/transitions, start/end frames, continuity rules]
```
