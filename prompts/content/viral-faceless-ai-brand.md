## When to use

Use these prompts when you want to establish, grow, and automate a high-volume, highly engaging faceless AI brand across social media platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, or X (Twitter). This system is designed to streamline the entire workflow—from psychological hooks to community building and automated high-output content pipelines.

## Prompts

### 1. Hook testing

Create a systematic testing matrix to find high-performing hook combinations using a multi-angle approach.

```text
You are an expert social media growth strategist and viral content engineer. I want to build a viral faceless brand on [platform].

Create a comprehensive 3x3x3 hook testing system for [niche]. 

Your output must include:
1. **3x3x3 Hook Framework**: 
   - 3 different angles/concepts (e.g., pain point, curiosity, contrarian).
   - 3 visual hooks (e.g., text overlay, fast-cut B-roll, green screen).
   - 3 pacing variations (e.g., hyper-fast, conversational, slow-reveal).
2. **CTR Targets**: Set specific click-through-rate (or scroll-stopping/hook-rate) targets for each variation based on industry benchmarks.
3. **Hold-Rate Analysis**: Define metrics and analysis workflows for the 3-second, 10-second, and 50%-completion hold rates on [platform].
4. **A/B Testing Tracker**: Provide a markdown or spreadsheet template to track and compare performance.
5. **Winner Scaling Workflow**: A step-by-step process to take the highest-performing hook combinations and scale them into full videos or multi-part series, utilizing our testing budget of [amount].
```

### 2. Faceless brand

Establish mascot rules, visual styling guides, and a consistent content framework for your niche.

```text
You are a creative brand architect and AI content expert. 

Build a complete, cohesive, and viral faceless AI brand for [niche] targeting [audience] with a goal of posting [posts/week] times.

Your plan must include:
1. **Mascot & Persona Rules**: Guidelines for an AI-generated avatar, mascot, or symbolic presence (visual description, personality, tone of voice, what they never do/say).
2. **Visual Consistency Blueprint**: Color palettes, font choices, aesthetic style, and specific Midjourney or Leonardo AI image generation prompts to keep all visual assets unified.
3. **Content Style & Formats**: The signature styles of content we will produce (e.g., aesthetic quotes, AI-narrated stories, visual loops, data visualisations).
4. **30-Day Posting Calendar**: A structured 30-day table specifying the topic, visual concept, audio/sound style, and caption hook for each day to hit our weekly goal.
```

### 3. Viral clip factory

Establish a rigorous grading rubric to isolate and refine the highest-potential clip concepts.

```text
You are a content analytics expert and professional video editor.

Create a viral clip scoring system for analyzing [video] (or long-form video content) to extract highly engaging clips for [platform].

Please build:
1. **The Scoring Rubric (out of 100)**: Break down how to rate each clip on:
   - **Hook** (0-20): Initial 3-second attention retention.
   - **Emotion** (0-20): Relatability, shock, humor, or empathy.
   - **Energy** (0-20): Pacing, visual dynamics, sound design.
   - **Value** (0-20): Practical advice, deep insight, or high entertainment.
   - **Quotability** (0-20): Soundbite-friendly, punchy statements.
2. **Scoring Formulas**: Mathematical guidelines or heuristic checklists to calculate a "Virality Index" to instantly identify which clips to post.
3. **Execution Templates**: Quick script and editing templates for top-scoring clips.
4. **Weekly Review Workflow**: A step-by-step process for analyzing performance data on [platform] each week to continuously refine the scoring weightings.
```

### 10. Infinite content loop

Scale content production safely without hitting platform flags, backed by editing blueprints.

```text
You are a high-volume content operations manager and media company founder.

Build a highly efficient, sustainable content system capable of producing 20-30 high-quality short-form videos weekly across multiple accounts with minimal human effort.

Your content machine blueprint must detail:
1. **The Production Workflow**: A streamlined step-by-step pipeline from ideation to script generation, bulk audio recording, batch video editing, and scheduled publishing.
2. **AI-Assisted Scripting Engines**: System prompts to generate scripts at scale while keeping high quality, emotional resonance, and brand distinction.
3. **CapCut Editing Blueprint**: Standardized editing rules for editors or automated tools (specifying caption styles, text transitions, visual effects, stock selection, sound effects, and background tracks).
4. **Scaling & Distribution Process**: Strategies for managing, scheduling, and optimizing distribution across multiple accounts/platforms without hitting spam filters.
```

## Tips

- **Platform Customization**: Customize pacing and aspect ratios for each platform—TikTok favors high-energy raw cuts, while YouTube Shorts prefers visual density and high resolution.
- **Dynamic Subtitles**: For faceless brands, captions are your voice. Use tools like CapCut or Submagic to generate high-contrast, animated captions with emojis to boost retention.
- **A/B Testing Discipline**: When running the Hook testing system, only change one variable at a time (either the voiceover hook, the background B-roll, or the caption overlay) to gather clear performance data.
