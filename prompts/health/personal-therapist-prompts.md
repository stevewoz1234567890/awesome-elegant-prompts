## Safety note

These prompts are for **reflection and journaling**, not diagnosis or treatment.

If you’re in danger or considering self-harm, contact your local emergency number or a crisis hotline immediately.

## Prompts

1. **Thought Pattern Analyzer (CBT-style)**

```text
You are a CBT-informed journaling coach (not a medical professional).

I’ve been thinking: [describe recurring thoughts].
Help me identify:
- possible cognitive distortions (catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking, etc.)
- underlying beliefs driving these thoughts
- evidence for and against them
- 3 healthier reframes I can practice

Keep it compassionate and practical.
```

2. **Decision Clarity Coach**

```text
I’m stuck on this decision: [describe situation and options].

Help me gain clarity:
- what values are in conflict?
- what fears are holding me back?
- what would I regret more in 10 years?

Walk me through a simple decision tree. End with 3 next actions I can take this week.
```

3. **Emotion Unpacker**

```text
I’m feeling [emotion] about [situation].

Act as an emotional intelligence coach:
- what might this emotion be signaling?
- what need isn’t being met?
- what physical sensations should I notice?
- what constructive actions can I take instead of reacting?
```

4. **Limiting Belief Challenger**

```text
I believe: [state limiting belief].

Challenge this belief:
- where might it come from?
- what evidence contradicts it?
- what is a more balanced alternative belief?

Give me 5 empowering replacement beliefs and 1 tiny experiment to test them.
```

5. **Relationship Pattern Revealer**

```text
Describe my last 3 significant relationship conflicts: [brief summaries].

Identify patterns:
- recurring dynamics
- my role in conflicts
- unmet needs
- possible attachment-style indicators (without diagnosing)

End with 3 boundaries or communication scripts I can try.
```

6. **Future Self Dialogue**

```text
I’m [current age] struggling with [issue]. You are my future self at [age + 10] who has improved this.

Write me a letter:
- what did you learn?
- what mattered most?
- what advice do you wish I followed earlier?

Be compassionate but honest.
```

7. **Anxiety Roadmap**

```text
I’m anxious about [situation].

Help me:
1) distinguish productive vs unproductive worry
2) identify what’s in my control vs not
3) create a concrete action plan for what’s in my control

Keep it short and structured.
```
